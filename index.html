Sortaria: Color Ball Puzzle

A calm ball sort puzzle for Android. Pour colored balls between tubes until every tube holds a single color.

  • Up to 12 colors, mystery "?" balls from level 8, a HARD level every 5th level from level 10.
  • Undo, restart, hints and an extra tube when you get stuck.
  • Color symbols mode, 12 languages, plays offline. No account needed; optional Google Play Games sign-in keeps your progress in the cloud.
  • Free to play with ads. Optional purchases: a one-time "Remove ads" and gem packs; gems are also earned by playing.
